## 🎮 What is CargoOpt 3D Packer?

**CargoOpt 3D Packer** is a 3D puzzle/management game where you act as a **Load Master** for an airline. Your mission is to efficiently load passenger luggage into an aircraft cargo hold.

### The Challenge
- 📦 **10 passenger bags** to load
- 🎯 **Red Zone** (1.05 m³) for passenger luggage
- 🚫 **No collisions** allowed
- ⚡ **No boundaries exceeded**
- 🏆 **Win condition**: All bags loaded!

### The Game
- 🎨 Beautiful 3D visualization
- 🖱️ Click-to-place interaction
- 📊 Real-time progress tracking
- 🎉 Victory celebration when complete
